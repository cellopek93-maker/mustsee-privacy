Common questions

How do I save a place or mark it as visited?
Open any place's detail screen and use the favorite (heart) and visited (checkmark) buttons at the top.

How does route planning work?
Pick 2 to 10 places in a city, tap Calculate Route, and MustSee builds a walking (or, for longer legs, driving) route with map and directions. You can edit the places, start a new route, or delete a saved route at any time from the route screen's menu.

Can I use the app offline?
Place details and your favorites, visited places, and notes are stored on your device. Loading new place photos and city catalog data, live routing, and events require an internet connection.

How do I change the app language?
Go to Settings and switch between English and Turkish; your choice is remembered.

How do I request a new city or report incorrect information?
Email us at the address above with the city or place name and what needs fixing — we review every message.
