MustSee Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

MustSee is a travel guide app. Most of what it does happens entirely on your device; the sections below explain the few things that don't, including the ads that keep the app free.

Accounts and personal information

MustSee has no user accounts, sign-in, or collection of personal details like your name or email. You don't need to share any information to use the app.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device location to show nearby places and route distances. This is processed on your device and is never sent to our own servers. If ad personalization is enabled (see Advertising below), Google AdMob may also use a coarse, approximate version of your location for ad delivery and measurement.

Favorites, visited places and notes

These are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

MustSee shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the app free. Where required, we ask for Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before showing personalized ads; if you decline, or later turn off ad personalization in your device's Settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising), ads continue to show, without personalization.

To deliver and measure ads, Google AdMob may process: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA — only if ATT permission is granted), other device information, coarse location, and app usage data. This data is handled by Google under its own advertising privacy policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we show a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ad personalization, as required by local regulation.

Directions

When you get directions to a place, MustSee opens Apple Maps, or — if you choose it and have it installed — Google Maps or Yandex Maps. Only the destination's name and coordinates are passed to that app; MustSee does not receive any data back from Google Maps, Yandex Maps, or Apple Maps.

Analytics

MustSee does not use Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, or any similar analytics or crash-reporting tool of its own. (Google AdMob's own measurement is covered under Advertising above.)

Photos and content

Place photos and catalog data are downloaded from our content servers (hosted on Backblaze B2 / Cloudflare). Like any web request, these servers may log standard technical information such as IP address and request time for security and operational purposes; this data is not linked to you and is not used for tracking.

Third-party apps

When you choose to open a place in Uber, Bolt or Yandex Go, you leave MustSee and those apps' own privacy policies apply.

Children

MustSee does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change.
