MustSee Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

MustSee is a travel guide app. We designed it to work without collecting personal data.

Accounts and personal information

MustSee has no user accounts, sign-in, or collection of personal details like your name or email. You don't need to share any information to use the app.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device location to show nearby places and route distances. Location is processed on your device only and is never sent to our servers or to third parties.

Favorites, visited places and notes

These are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

MustSee shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the app free. On first launch, we request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission to show personalized ads; if you decline, ads continue to show, without personalization. AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier and related usage data for ad delivery and measurement, under Google's own privacy policy.

Analytics

MustSee does not use Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, or any similar analytics or crash-reporting tool.

Photos and content

Place photos and catalog data are downloaded from our content servers (hosted on Backblaze B2 / Cloudflare). Like any web request, these servers may log standard technical information such as IP address and request time for security and operational purposes; this data is not linked to you and is not used for tracking.

Third-party apps

When you choose to open a place in Apple Maps, Google Maps, Yandex Maps, Uber, Bolt or Yandex Go, you leave MustSee and those apps' own privacy policies apply.

Children

MustSee does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change.
